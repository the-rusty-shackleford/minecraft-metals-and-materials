# D-0001 — One materials mod nested by its consumers; the gun mod aliases its old steel id

**Decision**: Steel lives here, as `metalsandmaterials:steel_ingot` with a nugget and a
block, tagged `c:ingots/steel`, `c:nuggets/steel`, `c:storage_blocks/steel` (and included
in the parent `c:` tags). Consumers nest this jar Jar-in-Jar and take the tags. The Ranged
Weapons Mod, which owned steel before, drops its item and registers an alias from
`rangedweaponsmod:steel_ingot` to ours, so items in inventories and chests survive the move.

**Why**: Rusty: "Perhaps the steel should be moved to a new elements mod so it can be easily
referenced from any mod". Vanilla Wheels needs steel by the ton and must not depend on a gun
mod. The gun mod's recipes already took `#c:ingots/steel`, so the move costs it only the item
and the recipe. The alias is the gun mod's to register because the old id is its namespace;
NeoForge resolves an aliased id to the new entry when the old is absent
(`DeferredRegister.addAlias`).

**Alternatives rejected**: *A second steel in Vanilla Wheels* -- two ingots that look the
same and do not stack. *Vanilla Wheels depending on the gun mod* -- a truck needing a pistol
mod. *Leaving steel in the gun mod and nesting the gun mod* -- the whole gun mod for one
ingot. *A datapack-only steel* -- items cannot come from datapacks.

`ModItems` and its 2.3.0 release notes.
